**Lasso v2.0.0 rebuilds the Decision Engine around the team's own triage rules.** It's a major version because two things change for anyone upgrading:

- **Two models are now required:** the arbiter model and the Tag Normalizer model. Ticket reviews and Tag Normalizer language-model runs won't start until both are set.
- **The engine classifies differently:** a rewritten taxonomy, a sixth outcome (`on_track`) and stricter code checks. Reviews saved before v2.0.0 and after it don't mean quite the same thing.

See **Upgrading from v1.0.0** before installing.

## Highlights

- **A taxonomy you can trace:** the prompt is rewritten from the team's taxonomy worksheet. Every answer cites trigger IDs (L1–L9, O1–O7, R1–R7, S1–S7), and the review detail shows each trigger's name beside its ID. #76, #77, #88
- **Rules enforced in code:** confidence floors, required triggers and verified re-engage attempts are checked after the model answers. Answers that fail are downgraded. #78
- **An arbiter for disagreements:** when the first answer and the confirmation disagree, an arbiter model judges their reasoning, then classifies the ticket itself. #78
- **Manual-triage case files:** a ticket sent to manual triage keeps how it got there. Reviewers can add Jira or Aha evidence and re-run it on the arbiter. #91
- **Reviewing undecided tickets:** step through the reviews still waiting for a decision, while embedding runs in the background. #74, #75

## What's new since v1.0.0

### Decision Engine: taxonomy and checks
- **Rewritten taxonomy:** based on the team's taxonomy worksheet (2026-10-28). #77
  - **Trigger IDs:** each category has numbered triggers, and the model reports the ones it matched in `signals_matched`.
  - **Concrete windows:** 48 and 72 business hours, and one or two sprints for Jira.
  - **Re-engagement:** the team's definition, and how to count attempts in the current stall. The model reports the attempts it counted by timestamp, in a new `reengage_attempts` field.
  - **On track:** a sixth outcome, `on_track`, for tickets that need nothing now.
  - **Escalation and Solve rules:**
    - **Churn:** needs an explicit statement from the customer.
    - **Leadership below 80 confidence, and silence after two attempts:** both go to the support owner with a "Leadership visibility:" note.
    - **Solve:** as soon as nothing remains, at 80 confidence. Zendesk's 7-day Solved window allows a reopen.
  - **Behaves as Expected** replaces "Not a Bug". A linked Jira or Aha item blocks Solve until a comment confirms its outcome.
  - **Time and status rules:** business hours, Pending auto-solve and On-hold.
- **Code checks:** run after every answer. #78
  - **Confidence floors:**
    - **Leadership:** 80. Below it, the ticket goes to the owner with a "Leadership visibility:" note.
    - **Solve:** 80.
    - **Owner and re-engage:** 70.
    - **Confirmation:** a confirmation below 75 doesn't count.
  - **Triggers:** an answer must cite one of its category's own trigger IDs, or it's downgraded.
  - **Re-engage attempts:** an attempt the model cites must be a public Support comment, or an internal note to an internal party. Two verified attempts escalate to the owner.
- **Arbiter:** when the first answer and the confirmation disagree, the arbiter (`CHEAPER_INFERENCE_ARBITER_MODEL_NAME`) does two things. #78
  - **Judging:** it weighs the two answers' reasoning without seeing the ticket.
  - **Classifying:** it then classifies the ticket itself.
  - **If its two rulings differ,** the ticket goes to manual triage.
- **Solved and Closed tickets** are no longer claimed for review, or counted as awaiting review. #78
- **Comment roles:** the ticket sent to the model labels each comment's author as support, Jira integration or internal, using known IDs. Before, every author but the requester was unlabelled. #76
- **Leadership roster:** Terry Brooks is added (Internal Projects, led by Rich Shupe). #76
  - **Roster reminders:** a review batch warns once the roster is a quarter past its last review. #79

### Decision Engine: manual-triage cases (#91)
- **Case files:** a review that ends in manual triage opens a case in a new `triage_cases` collection. The case records:
  - how the ticket got there: abstained, guard override, disagreement or engine error;
  - each model's answer;
  - the guard overrides;
  - the arbiter's judging reasoning;
  - the model's new `open_questions`.

  Thinking and raw replies go to S3 under `triage-cases/` when storage is set up, otherwise onto the case.
- **Reviewer evidence:** **Add Evidence…** on the Triage tab takes a file or pasted text. Only the fields read from it are saved, never the file, and evidence can't be edited or removed once added.
  - **Jira:** an XML export of the issue (the RSS format).
  - **Aha:** an idea or feature's History page, printed to PDF or copied as text.
  - **Refused:** files that can't be parsed, and oversized or malformed ones.
- **Re-run with Evidence:**
  - **The request:** sends the ticket, the case and its evidence to the arbiter. It's refused before any call if it would exceed about 150K tokens.
  - **The answer:** saved as a new review.
    - **A decision** resolves the case and feeds the weekly lessons summary.
    - **Manual triage again** keeps the case open with its new questions.
  - **Training data:** a re-run marked correct is exported as a training example.
  - **One at a time:** only one re-run of a case can run at a time.
  - **Interrupted re-runs** are finished without another model call, by the next re-run or when the Triage tab loads.
- **Recovery:** a case that couldn't be opened when its review was saved is opened when the Triage tab loads.
  - **Missed ones:** a sweep, at most every six hours, catches any that were missed.
  - **Older open cases:** the tab lists open cases past its 5,000-review limit (up to 1,000).
- **Weekly Review:** shows the cases resolved that week, and suggests lessons from them. A draft is refused if one of its cases was reopened after it was loaded.

### Decision Engine: review flow
- **Step through undecided reviews:** the review list and its details sit side by side. #75
  - **Previous/Next Ticket** skip reviews already marked correct or misclassified, or added to Zendesk.
  - **The table** loads up to the newest 5,000 reviews, not 200, with Decision and Added to Zendesk columns. Reviews with both are greyed out.
  - **Review Added to Zendesk** is recorded per review. Marking one review no longer hides the ticket's others.
- **Background embedding:** tickets are embedded on their own thread, so reviewing continues meanwhile. **Stop Embedding** finishes and saves the batch in flight. #75
- **Copy a review:** an icon button next to View on Zendesk copies the selected review's details. #74

### Decision Engine: settings and limits
- **New settings:** max tokens, effort and timeout are now settings in the Settings dialog, so tuning them needs no code change. #80
  - `DECISION_ENGINE_MAX_TOKENS`: the default rises from 4,096 to 16,000, since Claude Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 always think, and thinking counts against max tokens.
  - `DECISION_ENGINE_EFFORT`.
  - `DECISION_ENGINE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS`.
- **Unusable values** fall back to the default with a warning. #81, #86
  - **Max tokens** above 128,000.
  - **Timeouts** above 3,600 seconds, or `nan` or `inf`.
- **Review claims:** each lasts as long as every model call a ticket can make at the configured timeout, plus a margin. That's 45 minutes at the default, and never under 15. #81
  - **A/B claims** keep the timeout they were taken with. #86

### Data, evaluation and Jira links
- **Jira links:** #76, #92
  - **Import:** the Zendesk Jira links export is imported into a new `jira_links` collection, which ticket ingestion no longer overwrites.
  - **Write Tickets to MongoDB:** has an optional **Jira links file** row.
  - **Checks:** every record is checked before anything is written.
  - **Merging:** imports merge with links already stored, so several pages can be imported in any order.
  - **From the command line:**

    ```bash
    uv run python -m decision_engine.jira_links import <file>
    ```
- **Labelling tools:** #79
  - **`evaluation balanced`:** writes a sample with up to N tickets per category the engine last gave them, plus every leadership case, with the engine's answers hidden.
  - **`evaluation agree`:** reports agreement and Cohen's kappa between two labellers, listing the tickets they disagree on.
- **`authors candidates`:** lists author IDs that appear only on internal notes, so they can be labelled. #76

### Fixes
- **BERTopic training** no longer opens a second copy of Lasso in the packaged app. #89
  - **"Every ticket was left as an outlier" and "too few tickets":** these errors now give the minimum topic size and the ticket count. They also say how many tickets were left out for want of an embedding.
- **Tag Normalizer CLI:** it checks for its model before making the paid Voyage call. #87
- **Blank model settings:** a secondary, confirmation or arbiter model set to whitespace counts as unset. #88

### CI and dependencies
- **New dependencies:** `defusedxml` for Jira XML, and `pypdf` 6.21 or later for Aha PDFs. #91
- **Updated:** setuptools 84.2.1, langchain-core 1.7.0, xxhash 4.0.2 and jsonpointer 3.5. This replaces Dependabot's #68 and #82–#85. #93
- **Tag CLI test:** it no longer depends on a local `.env`. #90

## Upgrading from v1.0.0

- **Set the two required models** in Settings, or in `.env`. Lasso lists them at launch while they're unset. #87
  - **Arbiter model** (`CHEAPER_INFERENCE_ARBITER_MODEL_NAME`): **Review All** and review batches won't start without it. A/B tests don't need it.
  - **Tag Normalizer model** (`TAG_NORMALIZER_MODEL`): it no longer falls back to the primary model. The Tag Normalizer's language-model option asks for it before running.
- **Reinstall dependencies:** run `uv sync` to add `defusedxml` and `pypdf`.
- **Expect different answers:** the prompt version has changed, and the new rules downgrade answers that used to pass.
  - **Training exports** include only the current prompt version by default, so earlier answers are left out unless you choose older prompts.
  - **A/B comparisons** of runs from before and after v2.0.0 compare different rules.
- **New collections:** MongoDB creates these on first use, along with their indexes.
  - **`triage_cases`:** set `TRIAGE_CASES_COLLECTION` to rename it. Its name can't match any other Lasso collection.
  - **`jira_links`.**
- **Import Jira links** if you want them in the payload: use the new row on Write Tickets to MongoDB, or `decision_engine.jira_links import`.
- **Raise or check max tokens:** if you set `DECISION_ENGINE_MAX_TOKENS` below 16,000 for v1.0.0, thinking models may now run out of room.

## Known issues

- **Slow PDFs:** a PDF built to be slow to read can keep the Add Evidence dialog busy for a while. There's no timeout.
- **Reviews older than the limit:** the Triage tab lists only the newest 5,000 reviews, plus up to 1,000 older open manual-triage cases. Other older reviews aren't listed.
- **Unchanged from v1.0.0:**
  - **macOS builds aren't notarized:** Gatekeeper blocks them on other Macs until they're notarized, or opened with right-click > Open.
  - **Tag Normalizer:** language-model runs still can't be saved with **Save Run**.
  - **Zendesk retag scripts** have only been tested against a simulated Zendesk.
  - **Lint is still report-only in CI.**
- **Open decisions:** some Decision Engine items are still open (see *Known gaps* in the README).
