# Lasso v2.0.1

**Lasso v2.0.1 gives the Decision Engine a longer idle window for linked work items, and makes it aware of who each commenter is.** It also adds review filters, an Arbitration Results pop-up and a launch splash, and moves Lasso to Python 3.11.

See **Upgrading from v2.0.0** before installing: Lasso now needs Python 3.11, and the prompt version has changed.

## Highlights

- **Six-month idle window for linked work:** O2 and R4 now wait 6 months (182 calendar days), not one or two sprints, and they cover Aha feature requests as well as Jira issues.
- **Who is speaking:** the engine knows each staff commenter's department, title and seniority, and weighs what they say accordingly. #97
- **One row per ticket, and Arbitration Results:** the Reviews table shows each ticket's latest review. A new pop-up explains what an arbitration or re-run weighed and what to do next. #98
- **Review filters:** filter reviews by classification, decision, owner and Zendesk status. #100

## What's new since v2.0.0

### Decision Engine: taxonomy
- **Linked Jira and Aha items:** both triggers now apply to a linked Jira issue (`jira_links`) or an Aha feature request mentioned in the transcript.
  - **O2 (escalate to support owner):** a linked item with no meaningful action for **6 months**, up from 28 days (two sprints). It now names the product engineer *or product owner* who needs to be engaged. Its label is "Linked Jira or Aha item idle for 6 months".
  - **R4 (re-engage):** a linked item with no movement or comment for **6 months**, up from 14 days (one sprint). Its label is "Linked Jira or Aha item idle for 6 months".
  - **Same window:** O2 and R4 now share the same window. Escalation outranks re-engagement, so an idle item that meets O2 is escalated to its owner rather than re-engaged.
  - **Measuring the window:** it runs from the item's last `jira_integration` comment, or any other comment about it. If there are none, it runs from when the item was linked or first mentioned.
  - **Supersedes v2.0.0:** v2.0.0's "one or two sprints for Jira" windows no longer apply.
- **Who is speaking:** #97
  - **Staff roster:** Learnosity staff are listed by Zendesk user ID with department, title, seniority, and whether they've left. Each staff author's details are sent in the payload.
  - **Roles:** former Support staff count as Support for the comments they wrote while in Support. Other staff count as `internal`. A person's role is the one they held when they wrote the comment.
  - **Weighing statements:** the engine weighs what someone says by their area of responsibility and seniority. It never addresses former staff, and treats executives' comments as evidence that Leadership is aware.
  - **Responding counts only for the request itself:** a team counts as having responded only if its comment addresses the outstanding request or linked issue. An update on LRN-2 no longer hides an idle LRN-1.
  - **Customer success managers:** when there's a relationship risk, the engine says whether a customer success manager is involved.

### Decision Engine: reviews and arbitration
- **Latest review per ticket:** the Reviews table has one row per ticket, showing its newest review. Earlier reviews are listed in the detail pane, and an open manual-triage case stays on the ticket's row. #98
  - **Older open cases:** when a ticket's newest review resolves one case, an older open case keeps its Add Evidence and Re-run buttons.
- **Arbitration Results:** says whether the classification changed, what was weighed (model answers, arbiter reasoning, reviewer evidence, triggers) and the ticket's next steps. #98
  - **When it opens:** after a re-run, or after a single-ticket review decided by the confirmation model or the arbiter. It also has its own button.
  - **Comparison:** a re-run is compared with the review just before it, not the case's first review.
  - **Re-engage count:** the attempt count the code checks agrees with the engine's own count, and is saved on the review as `reengage_attempt_count`.
- **Request IDs:** each review's audit now records the proxy's request ID, which was always null before. This includes the arbiter's judging call. #98
- **Review filters:** four filters above the Reviews table (classification, decision, owner, Zendesk status) and a new Owner column. Next and Previous skip reviews the filters hide. Refreshing keeps the filters. #100
- **Trail uploads:** #95
  - **Where trails go:** triage trails upload under `_triage-cases/`. A trail that fails to upload stays in MongoDB with its error.
  - **Retry Trail Uploads:** moves trails that were kept back to S3, including ones on reviews still pending.
  - **Failed uploads:** the case detail shows each re-run whose trail upload failed.

### Interface
- **Launch splash:** shows while spaCy, BERTopic and the language model load, with a status line. Before, Lasso showed nothing for 20 seconds or more after launch. #96
- **Topic Modeling layout:** Ticket Data is on the left and the two models are on the right. #96
- **Logs window:** the log has its own window, opened with **Open Logs** or **Window > Logs** (Ctrl+Shift+L). It keeps recording while hidden. #95
- **Review layout:** the review list sits above the review detail, at full width, with a resizable divider. #95

### Fixes
- **Jira exports with @mentions:** these are no longer rejected as malformed XML. That includes UTF-16 exports. #99
- **High-DPI Windows displays** keep their scale factor with the splash screen. #96
- **Staff names:** Conor Walsh's Support-era comments are labelled Support again. The roster's Support names now match the Leadership recipients table. #97
- **Linux builds** no longer fail at the macOS-only installer step. #95

### CI and dependencies
- **Python 3.11:** Lasso now targets Python 3.11.
- **Security patches:** Lasso picks up the fixed releases of Pillow (13.0.0), dnspython (2.9.2) and filelock (4.2.9), which need Python 3.11. nltk moves to 3.10.4.
- **Other upgrades:** moving to Python 3.11 also brings in pandas 3 and numpy 2.4.

## Upgrading from v2.0.0

- **Install Python 3.11,** then run `uv sync` to rebuild the environment.
- **BERTopic models saved on 3.10 won't load on 3.11.** Lasso only writes these files and never reads them back, so the app itself isn't affected. A script that loads an older model would need Python 3.10.
- **Expect different answers:** the prompt version has changed.
  - **Idle linked items:** tickets with a linked item that's been idle for between 14 days and 6 months used to be re-engaged (R4) or escalated (O2). Now they aren't.
  - **Training exports and A/B comparisons:** by default, training exports include only reviews from the current prompt version, and A/B runs from before and after v2.0.1 compare different rules.

## Known issues
