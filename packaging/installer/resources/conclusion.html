Lasso is installed

Open it from your Applications folder or Launchpad. On first launch it offers to open its settings (Lasso > Preferences…, or ⌘,) for the MongoDB and model settings the Decision Engine needs. Topic modeling works without them.

If macOS says it can't verify the developer: Control-click Lasso in Applications, choose Open, then Open again. You only need to do this once.

To uninstall: drag Lasso from Applications to the Trash. Your settings stay in ~/Library/Application Support/Lasso until you delete that folder.
