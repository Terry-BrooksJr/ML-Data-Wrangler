Lasso

Lasso works with Zendesk ticket data: topic modeling (LDA and BERTopic), the Decision Engine's triage review, and the Tag Normalizer.

This installs Lasso.app into your Applications folder (about 1.2 GB, most of it the spaCy language model). It needs an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 12.3 or later, and an administrator password.

Your settings aren't part of the app, so installing a newer version keeps them.
